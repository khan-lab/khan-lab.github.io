# Historical Aziz Khan Blog import

Seven posts were migrated at the author’s request from the archived asntech website. Article files are under `content/blog/`; twelve images are local under `assets/blog/asntech/`. The original publication dates (2016–2019), titles, index summaries, tags, prose, quotations, code, captions and material links are retained. Each post explicitly references the existing `aziz-khan` member. `Archive` is the new required category; original tags remain unchanged.

The source repository is pinned to the revision recorded in `manifest.yml`. That manifest lists source HTML and destination checksums, original publication timestamps, public URLs and each image’s original/retrieval URL and checksum. Two missing images were recovered from public archive snapshots with payloads verified against the archive index’s SHA-1 digests. The Rhine photograph and Nature Figure 4 were recovered from their current publisher image paths. Image files were copied without pixel editing. Original figure credits and attribution links remain in the articles; media retain their applicable original terms.

Only article bodies were imported. Old theme navigation, sharing/analytics scripts and Disqus comments were excluded. Twitter quotes and their original links remain static, readable content rather than third-party embeds. Relative non-post links were resolved against the old site; the old website and its production repository were not modified. No old-domain redirects were added.

All seven HTML-to-Markdown conversions passed whitespace-normalized prose/code/quotation equivalence checks, with image alternative text preserved separately using explicit Quarto fig-alt attributes. Article dates are independent of the import date or archive-image snapshot dates. `image_cover: false` retains thumbnails without moving or duplicating the inline figures. The current site still controls whether citation appendices are enabled; article-body paper DOIs were not assigned as Blog-post DOIs.
