Anil is a Research Scientist in the Computational Biology and Cancer Regulatory Genomics (CBCRG) Lab at MBZUAI. He holds a PhD from the University of East Anglia, UK, and specializes in bioinformatics tool and pipeline development, genomic data analysis, and computational infrastructure.
